# Northstar Robotics 2026 Q2 Earnings Digest

Case type: fictional finance demo.
Prepared for: PSKA finance research demo.

## 中文摘要

北星机器人二〇二六年第二季度收入为十八点四亿美元，同比增长百分之十八。毛利率为百分之四十一点二，低于去年同期百分之四十四点七。经营现金流为一亿四千六百万美元，低于去年同期二亿一千一百万美元。未交付订单为六十三亿美元，同比增长百分之二十二。库存为七亿八千万美元，同比增长百分之二十五。回款天数达到七十一天，高于去年同期五十八天。

管理层强调未交付订单质量、软件附加率和下半年营运资金纪律。财务负责人表示，如果零部件交期稳定，库存正常化应从第四季度开始。

初步分析：本季度不是简单的“增长好、毛利差”。核心问题是，公司通过服务业务换取增长和客户留存，同时营运资金吸收了更多现金。后续需要追问服务扩张能否带来稳定收入，还是掩盖硬件订单转化放慢。

## Executive Facts

- Revenue was 1.84 billion USD, up 18 percent year over year and up 6 percent sequentially.
- Gross margin was 41.2 percent, down from 44.7 percent one year ago.
- Operating cash flow was 146 million USD, compared with 211 million USD one year ago.
- Backlog ended at 6.3 billion USD, up 22 percent year over year.
- Inventory was 780 million USD, up 25 percent year over year.
- Days sales outstanding reached 71 days, up from 58 days one year ago.

## Segment Notes

Cloud Robotics grew 25 percent year over year. Management attributed growth to warehouse automation deployments and higher software attach rates.

Industrial Automation grew 9 percent year over year. The segment saw slower order conversion in Europe and longer customer approval cycles.

Services grew 31 percent year over year. Services improved revenue visibility but lowered consolidated gross margin because field support costs were front-loaded.

## Management Commentary

The CEO emphasized backlog quality and software attach rates.

The CFO said working capital discipline is the main second-half priority. The CFO also said inventory normalization should begin in Q4 if component lead times remain stable.

## Preliminary Analyst View

The quarter was not simply "growth good, margin bad." The core tradeoff is that Northstar is buying growth and retention through services, while working capital is absorbing cash.

The main research question is whether service-led expansion creates durable recurring revenue or merely hides slower hardware conversion.
