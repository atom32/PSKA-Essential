# Management Action Ledger

Case type: fictional finance demo.

## 中文摘要

当前经营决定：二〇二六年下半年优先加强营运资金纪律。

原因：库存同比增长百分之二十五；回款天数从五十八天延长到七十一天；收入增长的同时，经营现金流反而下降。

下一步动作：每周复查用于项目交付的零部件库存账龄；把未交付订单拆成已签订单、等待验收订单和预测订单；要求大型服务合同写清毛利改善节点；把云端机器人软件附加率和续约客户单独跟踪；准备投资者问答，重点解释营运资金、订单兑现和服务毛利。

待确认记忆：财报调研时总是区分事实、推断和行动；分析硬件加服务型公司时，要同时跟踪回款天数、库存、未交付订单兑现、服务毛利和软件附加率。

## Current Operating Decision

Decision: prioritize working capital discipline in the second half of 2026.

Rationale:

- Inventory grew 25 percent year over year.
- DSO expanded from 58 to 71 days.
- Operating cash flow declined despite revenue growth.

## Actions

1. Weekly inventory aging review for deployment-bound parts.
2. Separate backlog into signed, acceptance-gated, and forecast-only buckets.
3. Require every large services deal to include margin ramp milestones.
4. Track Cloud Robotics software attach rate and renewal cohort separately from hardware revenue.
5. Prepare investor Q&A on working capital, backlog conversion, and service margin.

## Review Candidates

- Memory candidate: for finance research, always separate fact, inference, and action.
- Memory candidate: for hardware-plus-services companies, track DSO, inventory, backlog conversion, service margin, and software attach together.
- Trace candidate: link Q2 working capital analysis to future Q3 and Q4 follow-up.
