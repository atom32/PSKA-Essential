# Risk And Question Register

Case type: fictional finance demo.

## 中文摘要

北星机器人第二季度需要重点追踪五类经营风险：营运资金压力、毛利结构变化、未交付订单质量、欧洲区域转化放慢，以及客户集中度。库存增长和回款天数变长都快于收入增长，可能来自部署爬坡，也可能说明需求节奏或客户付款出现摩擦。

给财务负责人的关键问题包括：库存增加中有多少对应已签未交付订单，有多少只是安全库存；回款天数变长是由企业客户付款条件导致，还是项目验收推迟导致；第四季度服务毛利率预计能否改善；云端机器人收入中有多少软件附加期超过十二个月；未交付订单取消率是否仍低于历史平均。

证据标准：不要把单个指标直接变成结论。必须区分事实、推断和管理动作。

## Risks To Track

1. Working capital pressure

Inventory growth and DSO expansion both outpaced revenue growth. This can be benign during deployment ramps, but it can also indicate demand timing issues or customer payment friction.

2. Margin mix shift

Services growth is strategically useful, but lower near-term service margin can dilute consolidated gross margin. The question is whether software attach and renewal rates offset that pressure.

3. Backlog quality

Backlog growth is positive only if cancellation risk is low and if conversion timing remains predictable.

4. Regional slowdown

Industrial Automation saw slower Europe conversion. This may reflect macro timing rather than product weakness, but it needs follow-up.

5. Customer concentration

The top five customers represent 38 percent of trailing twelve-month revenue. Any deployment delay could move quarterly revenue and cash flow.

## Questions For CFO

- What portion of the inventory increase is tied to signed backlog versus speculative safety stock?
- How much of DSO expansion is caused by enterprise payment terms versus delayed acceptance milestones?
- What is the service gross margin exit rate expected for Q4?
- What percentage of Cloud Robotics revenue has software attach above 12 months?
- Are backlog cancellation rates still below historical averages?

## Evidence Standard

Do not turn one metric into a conclusion. Separate facts, inference, and management action.
